# SMART FAQ (from the Europress briefing, Nov 2026)

- **Connectivity:** each machine has a SIM card (4G/5G). Tilt sensor, GPS and movement sensors tell when the container is lifted and transported.
- **Electricity:** the compactor motor is about 3–5 kW and runs only during a compaction cycle — "peanuts" compared with a facility's other loads.
- **Compaction start:** a user always starts the cycle; safety rules prohibit automatic starting.
- **Fill prediction:** SMART predicts fill from compaction cycles, motor run time, real-time fill level and the hydraulic pressure curve, and tells you e.g. "no pickup needed in the next 7 days".
- **Weight:** fill level correlates with weight but is not weight. Options today: platform scale under the machine (accurate, costs about as much as the compactor) and the Scaletipper bin tipper with dynamic weighing (1 kg accuracy). A virtual weight from sensor data is being developed; it is indicative, not legal-for-trade.
- **Remote service:** SMART Center technicians connect remotely, see sensor and electrical states and solve many faults (e.g. blown fuses) without a visit.
- **E-stop** = emergency stop.
- **Pricing of SMART:** service fee only, no up-front charge.
- **Contracts:** customers usually sign 3-year contracts with a waste operator and then tender. Europress is not part of that contract; SMART gives the customer visibility to compare.
- **ERP:** SMART integrates with the waste operator's ERP or the property manager's system.
- **Seasonality:** Europress has no long-term measurements of how density changes between winter and summer — the dashboard's density-by-month chart is the first view of it.
