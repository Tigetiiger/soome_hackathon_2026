Europress SMART — business model

Europress × Since AI Hackathon 2026 — business side. Companion to the technical documentation. Detailed version: SMART Weight & Insight. Demo on synthetic data; all prices are hypothetical. Built 2026-11-07.

Europress builds compactors and balers, and SMART connects them. We turn the data SMART machines already send into the weight of the waste — no new firmware, no new hardware — and use it to win more SMART subscriptions. The customer pays a share of the money SMART predicts it saves. Waste-operator partners get a driver plan in return for sharing pickup, weight and invoice data through SMART.

0Summary of the whole submission

  • Problem. Weight decides what a customer pays, but only the waste operator knows it, after the truck has left. About 15 % of Europress customers are on SMART.
  • What we built. A weight model that reads the data SMART machines already send (fill, compaction, hydraulic pressure, weather): about 5.3 % off on machines it never saw, with €0 extra hardware. On top of it, one dashboard with three views.
  • Customer. Sees the approximate weight in every container and the expected charge before the truck comes, plus the savings SMART AI found. Demo hotel group: €52,154 a year, 37 % of its waste cost.
  • Waste-operator partners (~20). Share pickups, weights and invoice lines through SMART; get a driver plan, pickups 14 days ahead and the load before the truck leaves. Five demo partners: 3,945 fewer truck trips a year.
  • Europress. More SMART subscriptions. SMART is paid with a share of the money it predicts it saves, agreed per customer (demo 25 % ≈ €311k a year on the demo fleet). Free is the bare minimum and fully manual: no weight, no fill levels, nothing automatic.
  • Trust and control. Europress never sells data to third parties; data is only exchanged when both parties agree.
  • Next step. The model is trained on a simulated fleet today. After the demo, Europress may need to invest in weighing the real output of a small group of compactors, so the model can be retrained on real weights (section 7).

1The problem

  • Weight decides the bill, but only the operator knows it — at the weighbridge, after the truck has left.
  • Customers can't see what their next pickup will cost, and trucks often come before the machine is full.
  • Operators don't know how many drivers they need next week; Mondays carry the whole weekend.
  • Europress sells the machines, but only about 15 % of customers are on SMART today.

2What we built

A weight model. It reads what a SMART machine already reports — fill level, compaction strokes and hydraulic pressure, plus the local weather — and gives the approximate weight in the container now and at the next pickup. Weight × the customer's price per tonne gives the expected charge before the truck comes. For anything about weight, the technical documentation is the reference.

More SMART subscriptions, a recurring fee that grows with customer savings, and sales leads ranked by savings.

€1.25M of yearly customer savings; at a 25 % share, about €311k a year in SMART fees.

Operators are partners. The customer sees an approximate weight and what SMART can improve — SMART never tells a customer that the operator's figure is wrong.

4How the money is made

Price: a share of the money SMART predicts it saves, agreed per customer. The fee is always smaller than the saving, so the customer stays. The demo uses 25 %: the hotel group would pay about €13,000 a year and keep about €39,000.

Table 2. Free and SMART.

FreeSMART
Weightnone — weight is what SMART sellsevery container, now and at the next pickup
Moneycost and pickup count; the total saving SMART would find, lockedevery saving, plus the expected charge of each pickup
Machineslist and timetable pickups only — no fill levelfill level, when each machine is full, history, patterns and forecasts
Ordering and alertsmanual: the customer orders each pickupautomatic: SMART orders the truck when the machine is full and alerts on savings
Period and AIlast 1–3 months; 3 AI questions a dayany period; no limit

Where the money comes from:

  1. Conversion. Free shows a customer what it is missing — its weight and its next bill — and SMART unlocks it.
  2. Size of the saving. The fee grows with the saving, so sales start with the biggest savers: large customers save a median €60,612 a year (28 % of cost).
  3. Partners. Every operator that shares data sharpens the predictions for all its customers and brings them to SMART. There are only about 20 operator partners in Finland, against many customers.
  4. Retention. A fee that is a share of a real saving is easy to keep paying.

6Trust and control

Europress does not sell data to third parties. Data is only exchanged, and only when both parties agree: the customer and its operator share through SMART so that pickups, drivers and bills can be planned. What Europress sells is trust and control.

7Next step: train on real weight

Today the weight model is trained and tested on a simulated fleet of 400 machines. To prove its accuracy on real compactors, and to train it on real waste, Europress may need to invest in weighing what a small group of compactors actually outputs. Only that reference group needs a scale; every other SMART machine keeps using the model, by software.

Table 4. Ways to get real weights, cheapest first.

source of real weightwhat it takescost
Weighbridge weights from operator partnerspartners share them through SMART, as part of the data exchangeno hardware
Europress's own scale platform or Scaletipperfit products Europress already sells under a reference group of machinesEuropress's own product price
Load cells under a reference group4 sealed load cells under the supports of about 10 machines, reporting into SMART≈ €3.4–6.3k per machine installed, ≈ €34–63k for ten (estimate)
  1. Weigh every pickup from the reference group, across materials and seasons (wet autumn, frozen winter).
  2. Retrain the model on those real weights, and test it on machines outside the reference group.
  3. Roll out the retrained model to every SMART machine as a software update; the rest of the fleet needs no hardware.
  4. Keep the group weighing, so the model is checked and retrained as machines and waste change.

The investment is made only after the demo has convinced customers and partners, and it is sized to the reference group, not to the fleet.

9Assumptions

  • All data is synthetic (Europress competition pack and our simulator); all prices are hypothetical. "Data is demo" is shown in the app.

  • Customer machine weights are computed on a simulated machine of the same material, size and fill.
  • Driver plan: one driver handles about 6 container pickups a day.
  • About 15 % of customers on SMART and about 20 operator partners: as stated by Europress.
