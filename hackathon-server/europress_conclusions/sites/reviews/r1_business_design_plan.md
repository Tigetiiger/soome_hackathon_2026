## 2. Five biggest visual problems

1. **Colour has no single meaning (all sections; `1280light_gaps`, `_chain`, `_offering`, `_top`).** Green `#1a7f4b` is simultaneously "Europress node" (chain), "paid chip" (offering), "device weight" (gaps bars, ladder monthly band), "pass" (verdict) and "ok" note. Red is "operator" (chain node outline), "money flow" (chain dashed edge), "median €0", every gate card, the fee bar in the calculator and the σ10 detection line. Navy fills the three hero nodes although the system reserves it for chrome. The DS rule is device = action blue, ticket = ink, invoice = grey, red only for invoice errors/negative NPV. Root cause: the page keeps its own 50-line `:root` from the marketing skin and overrides it twice instead of consuming `theme.css`.
2. **Hero "Three weights, one truth" does not read at a glance (`1280light_top`, `1280dark_top`).** Three navy boxes, three differently styled edges, five 10.5 px annotations scattered around a decorative person-in-a-circle icon. The eye lands on the icon, which carries no information. In dark mode the annotation text is hard-coded `#1f2429` and disappears (visible in `vp_1280_dark_top.png`).
3. **Value chain uses four node colours and three edge colours (`1280light_chain`, `400light_chain`).** Green/red/grey node outlines are not in the legend; money is a red dashed curve (reads as "error"); edge labels are 11–12 px blue text floating over white. The formula label under the diagram is longer than any node.
4. **Offering matrix is ragged and loud (`1280light_offering`).** Solid green "paid" blocks are the highest-contrast objects on the page; "bundled" is a 45° hatch; two of four buyer columns are almost empty with dotted left borders that look like broken rules; chips sit at different vertical positions within a row. At 400 the matrix becomes a 1 900 px list with repeated EVERYONE/CHAINS eyebrows.
5. **Uneven card heights leave holes (`1280light_gaps`, `_roadmap`, `_economics`).** `#gaps` left-bottom card ends 250 px above its row, right-bottom 300 px; the roadmap's three KPI tiles and the company-context tiles are 60 % empty; `.kill` gate cards have `height:auto` but their row stretches to the longest card.

Also noted: ladder legend "per-lift error" and "fleet model (literature)" are the same blue (`1280light_weigh`); roadmap first phase label is clipped to "des" (`1280light_roadmap`); `.sg.open` uses a gradient (DS: none); calculator label says "autumn bar / navy line" while the bar is red and the line black; tags are 700 weight (DS 500); border radii 6–14 px and 9999 px pills remain in `.note`, `.chip`, `.kill`, `#tip`, `.meter`, `.hbar .track`, `.presets`, `.skip`.
