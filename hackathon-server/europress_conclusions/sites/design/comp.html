Weight add-ons for Europress SMART

Three weights, one truth

Europress compactors already know how full they are. With a firmware upgrade they can also estimate how heavy they are, for €0 of extra hardware. Operator tickets keep that estimate honest, and Europress's own scale platforms supply ground truth. Inside SMART, that weight becomes a neutral second opinion on every ticket and every invoice.

The CFO insight

Subscription alone is negative over six years. Add an assumed +5 pp service-contract renewal uplift and higher SMART attach, and the base case turns positive. One won renewal is worth about €5 k machine margin plus six years of service margin. Assumption

"Weight makes SMART and the service contract stickier, which is where Europress makes its profit" — not "a new revenue line".Board framing, business plan §5
