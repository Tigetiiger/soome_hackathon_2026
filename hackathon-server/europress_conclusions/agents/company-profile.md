## 4. Gaps and questions to verify with Tommi

1. **Tiers.** What exactly is in Intro, Insights and Integrated today, and what are the list prices (€/device/month)? None of this is public. Is "SMART FullService" (Tamro) a fourth package?
2. **Scale Solutions.** How many scale platforms and Scaletippers are installed, what is their accuracy, are they legal-for-trade (OIML/MID), and what do they cost? Can their data serve as **ground truth** for the virtual scale?
3. **Existing "filling weight" display.** Tamro and Citycon "monitor filling weights / loads". Is that already a pressure-based kg estimate? If so, the virtual scale is an **upgrade**, not a new feature, and we should say so.
4. **Group numbers.** Is ~€70M consolidated group revenue for 2025 or 2026, or a gross figure? Which entities are included (Benelux, Australia partner)? What share comes from service and SMART?
5. **Poland.** Is there a factory, or a planned one? No public record shows one; Europress Poland files as a sales agent. What is the Polish installed base, and which operators (Alba…)?
6. **Leasing.** Who are the financing partners, and does the ~6-year lease bundle service and SMART? A 2024 HUS tender split purchase, **leasing**, refurbished units and separate service contracts [F ted.europa.eu notice 646936-2024].
7. **Operator API terms.** Who owns the data today, and can Europress resell an operator feed without breaching contracts?
8. **Installed and connected base.** How many machines in total, how many SMART-connected, and how many Retrofit units on other brands?
9. **Predictive-maintenance launch.** What is its scope and timing? Nothing is public yet.
10. **Benefit claims.** What is the basis for the −40% cost and +58% fill figures, so the business case can cite them safely?

**Unverified or not found:** a Danish or Baltic entity; acquisitions in 2023–2026 (none found); the Wastequip and Stadler digital offers; public SMART pricing.
