# A's critique of B and C

## B: workbench

**Strongest**
1. **It keeps the denominator.** Cards act as filters, so "77% weighbridge-side" opens to its rows. Best for traceability (20%) and customer distrust.
2. **It shows cross-device patterns.** OP-E-V16's tare offset hits 83 lifts on 56 devices (B's count).

**Flaws**
1. **It is not "a panel that draws conclusions".** It lands on a 4,646-row grid with z-scores. Facility managers bounce, so value (25%) and clarity (5%) suffer.
2. **It demos poorly in 3 minutes.** The screenshot is a wall of rows.
3. **Its R1 rule differs from the engine's.** B flags when the invoice is outside the band against both masses. The engine requires device and ticket to *agree* first. The top row, COL-00304-0017 (device −34% vs ticket), is still labelled "Invoice ≠ both masses".

**Steal:** filter-cards with live counts, and bulk-select → one proposal per counterpart.

## C: embedded

**Strongest**
1. **It is literally "an addition to the SMART thing".** NEW tags plus an on/off toggle make integration (15%) and productisation (10%) visible.
2. **The conclusion sits on the object, with a ballpark band.** "4,200 kg ± 118" is Tommi's "ballpark is enough".

**Flaws**
1. **Conclusions are scattered.** There is no "what to do this week" view. Fleet € and cause split have no home.
2. **It redraws Europress's own UI in front of Europress.** A mismatch costs credibility, and five surfaces is too much to build by Sunday 10:00.
3. **"Ticket outside band" is drawn in red.** That implies operator blame and breaks the operator-neutral anchor.

**Steal:** the toggle and NEW tags, the device-card weight strip as an entry point, and the SMART Center approval lane.

## A's own weaknesses
- The rank mixes units (the hero invoice sits 5th), and 8 cards can look curated.

## Recommended hybrid
1. **One panel, conclusions first.** Verified Weight is a single SMART nav entry that opens on the ranked findings feed. Each card has a sentence, € p10–p90, chips and a CTA. Rank "actionable now" first, then annualised €.
2. **Every card is a filter.** "Show the rows" opens B's grid pre-filtered (25 R1 / 18 vehicles / 45 of 78). Bulk approval lives there.
3. **The detail page is the proof:**
   - three neutral-coloured masses
   - the math and evidence rows
   - "what could make this wrong"
   - approve → `approval.decided`
4. **C as two hooks, not five redraws.** A weight strip with a one-line conclusion on the device card, deep-linking into the feed, and an approvals lane in SMART Center.
5. **Tiers gate cards on the same feed:**
   - Intro: one teaser card
   - Insights: R2–R6 and Proof of Fullness
   - Integrated: R1, bulk approval and the operator API
   - The "says no" card and the rules page appear in every tier.
