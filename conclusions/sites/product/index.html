Weigh at source. Check at the invoice.

Your compactor already knows how full it is. Verified Weight gives it a weight estimate with an honest ± band, puts it next to the weighbridge ticket and the invoice, and explains any gap without blame. You approve every action.

How it works

Start free. Add accuracy only where it pays.

Three accuracy levels on things Europress already sells. Tommi's rule applies: ballpark is enough for most decisions, so the default costs €0 in hardware.

±3–5% monthly

  • €0 BOM: uses the existing operator API
  • Per-lift error stays near ±12% until pressure-curve features exist (see chart)

Good enough for: quarterly invoice assurance against +10% inflation.

Good enough for: per-lift disputes, tenant billing.

Observed on the dataset's simulated integrated scale (σ 1.4% + 15 kg), i.e. T2-grade. At Tommi's ±10% ballpark, a +10% overbilling is caught 17% per lift, 62% per quarter Simulated.

What could make this wrong

No peer-reviewed study shows mass-from-pressure on compactors. The weighbridge reference itself is only ±40–80 kg (OIML R76). The T0 figures are a literature-based error budget until a 10-machine pilot measures them (Bland-Altman, leave-one-machine-out, ticket-count ablation; accept if monthly error ≤6% and per-lift p90 ≤20%).

What could make this wrong

The stored-tare effect might be a simulator artefact. The split-half check shows each vehicle's offset is identical in both halves of the window, which is what a stale stored value looks like, but real fleets change equipment and fuel load. Findings go to the operator first, as a private hygiene report.

  Limits and what remains untested
  
    What we do not claim
    
  
    
    
    Limits
      
      
    • All data is synthetic. The dataset's device weight is a simulated integrated scale (σ 1.4% + 15 kg), better than our T0 would be.
      • 
      
    • T0/T1 accuracy is untested. ±12–15% per lift and ±5–6% monthly are a literature-based error budget; no peer-reviewed mass-from-pressure study exists for compactors.
      • 
      
    • All € are hypothetical tariffs, not savings. Task C/C2 values are simulated expected values, not causal evidence.
      • 
      
    • Contamination and moisture detection does not work on this data. Our density proxy reaches ~1% precision (). It needs pressure-curve shape from future firmware: roadmap, not a feature.
      • 
      
    • The stored-tare effect may be a simulator artefact; it is stable in both halves of the window, but only a pilot can confirm it.
      • 
      
    • Verification, not billing. Billing on device weight would fall under NAWI 2014/31/EU legal metrology.
      • 
      
    • No pickup routing or timing. SMART already does that; Proof of Fullness is evidence for schedule changes only.
      • 
      
    • Freedom-to-operate check needed (e.g. US8794135B1, US11162834B2).
    
    
    Untested, and how a pilot tests it
      
      
    • Per-lift and monthly T0 error: 10-machine golden cohort on scale platforms, ~150–240 lifts, Bland-Altman + leave-one-machine-out.
      • 
      
    • How many tickets T1 needs: ablation over k = 0/3/5/10/20.
      • 
      
    • Interval coverage: conformal 80% bands should cover 75–85%.
      • 
      
    • Operator acceptance of operator-first findings; at least one operator sharing tickets via the API.
      • 
      
    • Willingness to pay ≥ €12/device/month at 1–2 chain customers.
